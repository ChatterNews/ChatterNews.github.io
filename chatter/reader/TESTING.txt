ORBIT — CHROMEBOOK PILOT

Start
1. Extract the ZIP and keep the entire Orbit folder together on your USB.
   Make each separate student drive from a fresh ZIP extraction, before opening
   Orbit. A copy of an already-used folder keeps that same newsroom identity.
2. Open Start Orbit.html, then choose Open Orbit. The reader is at
   https://delsaint18.github.io/orbit-reader/
3. Choose Open Story Drive and select the whole Orbit folder. Allow the reader
   to read and save there. Wait for preparation to finish.
4. Bookmark the reader. After preparation you can reopen it offline, select the
   drive, and start Orbit. First preparation needs internet for the small reader.

Your work
Save story and Finish session write verified editable work into Chatter News.
Use Finish session before closing the tab or signing out. Wait for the success
message, return to the reader, and eject the USB through the Chromebook Files app.
Separate room Download/export buttons use Chrome's normal download destination.
The editable session archives include linked story work and media.

Orbit also keeps recovery files on this Chromebook. These do not travel with
the USB. Open a saved .chatter file when continuing on another computer.
Each drive has its own recovery workspace. Only one tab may edit a drive at once.

School setup
No Linux, extension, Terminal, or local server is required. School policy must
allow the reader website, microphone/camera when used, and selected-folder access.
If browser storage is erased at sign-out, prepare the reader and copy the app
again at the next session. A bookmark alone does not preserve an offline copy.

Try on a representative Chromebook
Start online, prepare, disconnect Wi-Fi, then reopen the bookmarked reader.
Open Studio, play and duplicate a MIDI clip, change notes and the mixer, and
record if a microphone is available. Finish session, inspect Chatter News,
then reopen a saved story on another computer. Try another drive and verify
that its stories and badges stay separate. Report any prompt or failed step.

Status
This is a pilot package. Hardware recording, offline model inference, USB
removal, and school sign-out policies still need an actual Chromebook test.
Supporting files are inside _Orbit. Do not remove or rearrange them.
